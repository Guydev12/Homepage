Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe

Bake the perfect chocolate chip cookies with this easy-to-follow recipe. Crispy on the outside, soft and gooey on the inside – a treat everyone will love!

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon of salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) of unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup of granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup of packed brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups of semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a small bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a larger bowl, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla extract until creamy.
  4. Add eggs, one at a time, and beat well after each addition.
  5. Gradually add the flour mixture, mixing until fully incorporated.
  6. Stir in chocolate chips.
  7. Drop by rounded tablespoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheets.
  8. Bake for 8-10 minutes or until golden brown.
  9. Cool on baking sheets for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely.

Fun Tips & Variations

  • Use dark chocolate chips for a richer flavor.
  • Add a pinch of cinnamon for a subtle twist.
  • Sprinkle a little sea salt on top before baking for a sweet and salty balance.
